Parent-friendly summary: Kiki Coloring saves artwork and preferences primarily on the device. The app also includes services used for app stability, analytics, configuration, content delivery and purchases. Those services may process limited technical or transaction information as described below.

01

Overview and scope

This Privacy Policy explains how HDS Group / HDS Consultancy ("HDS", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the Kiki Coloring mobile application (the "App"). It applies to the App and related support communications. It does not replace the privacy terms of Google Play, Firebase, the device operating system or an application selected by a user to share exported artwork.

Kiki Coloring is designed as a child-friendly creative application. A parent or guardian should review this Policy and supervise purchases, support requests, exports and sharing.

02

Information we handle

The categories below reflect the App's current declared functionality and software dependencies. The exact information processed can vary by device, operating-system settings, App version and whether a particular feature is enabled or used.

App activity and preferences

Selected themes and pages, favourites, content-access state, downloaded-content status, and similar App preferences may be stored on the device. If analytics is enabled, limited interaction events may also be processed to understand feature usage and improve the App.

Technical and diagnostic information

App version, device and operating-system information, language, approximate region derived from network information, Firebase installation identifiers, crash reports, performance or error details, and technical logs may be processed by Firebase services when those services are active.

Purchase information

Product identifiers, purchase or restoration status, transaction identifiers, verification data and entitlement state may be processed to deliver or restore paid content. Payment-card details are handled by Google Play, not stored by HDS.

Communications

If a parent or guardian contacts us, we may receive their email address, name if provided, message content, attachments and related support correspondence.

03

Children's privacy

Kiki Coloring is intended to provide a safe, creative experience for children. The App is not designed to ask a child to create an HDS account or directly submit their name, email address, telephone number, precise location or other contact details to HDS.

Parents and guardians should handle support enquiries, purchases and sharing. We do not knowingly use information from children for personalised advertising or sell children's personal information. Technical services included in the App may process limited device, usage or diagnostic data as described in this Policy and under their own terms. If you believe a child has provided personal information to HDS, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

04

Local artwork storage and sharing

Colouring progress, drawing strokes, fills, stickers, favourites and saved artwork are designed to be stored locally in the App's storage area on the device. HDS does not routinely receive the content of locally saved artwork merely because it is created or saved.

When a user chooses to export or share artwork, the App creates an image file and passes it to the operating system's share interface. The user then chooses the destination application or service. That destination processes the exported file under its own privacy policy. Parents or guardians should supervise sharing and review the destination before sending any artwork.

05

Google Play Billing

Purchases and purchase restoration on Android are processed through Google Play Billing. Google may collect and process payment information, account information, transaction details and device or fraud-prevention information under Google's own terms and privacy policy.

HDS does not collect or store full payment-card numbers, card security codes or other payment-card credentials. HDS may receive or retain limited purchase information needed to confirm entitlement, such as the purchased product, transaction status, a purchase token or transaction identifier, and the time an entitlement was verified.

07

When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary:

  • to providers that operate App functions described in this Policy;
  • when a user deliberately exports or shares artwork to a selected application;
  • to comply with law, court orders or valid government requests;
  • to protect users, the public, HDS, or the security and integrity of the App; or
  • as part of a merger, acquisition, financing or transfer of the App, subject to appropriate privacy protections.
08

Retention and deletion

Locally stored artwork and preferences remain on the device until they are deleted through an available App function, the App's storage is cleared, or the App is uninstalled. Exported files remain wherever the user chose to save or share them and must be deleted from those locations separately.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer the request, maintain necessary business records, resolve disputes and comply with law. Purchase records are retained by Google according to Google's requirements. Firebase data is retained according to the configuration and retention periods applicable to each Firebase service.

A parent or guardian may request access to or deletion of personal information held directly by HDS by emailing hello@hdsgroupmy.com. We may need to verify the request. HDS cannot delete records controlled solely by Google Play, another share destination or a device owner.

09

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information handled by HDS. Local data also depends on the security of the device and operating system. No storage or transmission method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Parents and guardians should keep devices updated, use device access controls, supervise sharing and avoid sending sensitive information in support messages.

10

We may update this Policy when App features, SDKs, legal requirements or data practices change. The revised version will be posted at this URL with a new "Last updated" date. Material changes may also be communicated through the App or its store listing where appropriate.
